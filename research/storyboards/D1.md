## Close (short)
**So what is delta hedging?** — holding index against an option so that the next small move does not matter, and re-balancing after every move so that the *next* small move does not matter either. The book then earns the clock and pays for the bend; the two tie exactly when the index wobbles at the vol the option was sold at, and because you can only re-balance now and then, even a tie leaves a small, random, shrinking-with-frequency leftover. One formula at most: daily edge ≈ ½ Γ F² (σ²_sold − σ²_realised) × δt. Collapsed: Hull's bank (S = 49, 100,000 shares, $240,000, the two 20-week tables ending at $263,300 and $256,600, Table 14.4's 0.43 → 0.09); Natenberg's futures ledger (+62.81 against 63.00); the hedging-error rule of thumb sd ≈ √(π/4) · vega · σ/√n and why (n re-hedges are an n-sample estimate of the variance); the "which vol do you hedge with" aside in two paragraphs (hedge at the real vol and the total is locked but the marks are noisy; hedge at the implied and each day is smooth but the total is path-dependent — Wilmott ch. 12); transaction costs as one sentence (hedge more often and the leak shrinks but the costs grow; the optimum is interior). "Where it is used": every desk's daily P&L explain line; D2 (the break-even by strike is the smile); C5 (the residual after clock and bend has names); E2 (the thousand weeks are Monte Carlo). Last line, no formula; pull to D2 ("Raj sold 15.9 %; the 23,050 call was sold at 15.5 % and the 22,250 at 17 % — why would the same index wobble differently depending on the strike?").

## Why this shape
- A real week with a known ending makes the bet honest and the game short: seven presses, every number a close the reader could look up.
- The "never hedge" toggle is the naked bank from Hull in one column instead of a figure.
- One formula, under the bars it summarises, only after the bars have been seen for a week.
- The histogram answers the only question the real week cannot: whether what happened was luck. Nothing else from drwong's lab is kept (no carry/cost bands, no residual plug, no MC button without a sentence).

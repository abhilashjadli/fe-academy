**Working title:** *The line is half the answer*
**Standfirst:** Fit a line through a cloud of points and you get two things: a slope, and a number that says how sure you are. The slope is usually fine. The "how sure" is a lie more often than you'd think — and you can see the lie in the leftovers.
**Assumes:** you can read a scatter plot and know what an average is. That is all.
**Characters:** Funnel, Echo, the Twins and the Drunks — four line-drawn "liars" (faces via the Comic component) who each break one assumption. Plus Ada, who checks the leftovers.

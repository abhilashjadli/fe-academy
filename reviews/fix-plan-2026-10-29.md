# Fix plan after the three reviews (2026-10-29)

Sources: `reviews/pattern-review.md`, `reviews/B4-review.md`, `reviews/A3-review.md`. Sponsor's question that triggered them: "are we brainlessly following the same build-up pattern; are the illustrations the best suited; are we comparing with what exists?"

## Style-guide amendments (adopted)
1. The four closing sections are **optional**. Mandatory: one plain "So what is X?" paragraph after the mechanism, one formula at most. "Use it yourself" only when the use is a different object from earlier figures. "Where it is used" is three sentences, not three cards, naming at most one later chapter.
2. **A clip never duplicates the figure that follows it.** It is the opener's reveal, a "watch" tab *inside* the figure it belongs to, or a morph beat. "Watch first (N seconds)" as a separate figure is retired.
3. **Characters earn their place per chapter.** Interstitial `Says` lines are banned unless the line states a belief the reader could hold and the next figure breaks it. Mel is right at least once in the library. Faces are legend swatches inside figures, not mascots between them.
4. **The reveal is a picture, and "real" means all of it.** A result card sits under a drawing. "Real"/"artefact" only when every series is real; the lesson a figure reveals is never a parameter the author typed in.
5. **≤ 2 controls and ≤ 3 readouts per figure; no two chapters share a closing label.** Each chapter writes its own last line and its own pull; neither contains a formula.
6. **Three arc shapes beyond the bet-led one**: artefact-first-rebuild (C1, C4, D2, H2); game-as-spine (B2, D1, A4); two-pictures-one-clock (B3, B6, C2, F1). C5 is pure illustrated text.
